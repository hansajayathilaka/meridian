**One honest correction to the spec's literal wording, found and fixed during this task (task
8.17), and flagged again by that task's own review round:** messages 2 and 3 do **not** arrive on
this same connection. `drain_mailbox` (`apps/rendezvous/src/ws.rs`) fires only once, at connect
time, and `accept_request` does not trigger an on-demand re-drain — so, for a **brand-new** contact
specifically, the second and third queued messages (drained in the same connection, before the
accept decision) hit `ChatError::RequestPending` and are held back, not delivered, until the next
reconnect. Before task 8.17 they were then silently, permanently deleted — acked despite never
reaching the user. After the fix, they survive, unacked, and redeliver correctly on the *next*
reconnect:

## Part 2 — cross-federation: Org A sender → Org B mailbox → reconnect

Per this phase's own architect consult (point 2) and tasks 8.6/8.13's own reviewed finding: Org A's
real `route_foreign` always pre-checks Bob's live reachability at Org B *before* attempting the
actual federated route (architect decision #3). A recipient who never connects to Org B at all is
therefore `not_connected` **at the sender**, never a queued outcome — `handle_fed_route`'s
offline-enqueue branch on Org B is structurally unreachable through the live, uninterrupted
client-to-client wire path for that case. This is not a gap in this demo; it is the same structural
fact task 8.6's and 8.13's own reviewed work already established. The honest proof for the
cross-org case is Org B's own mailbox — inspected here against Org B's real, live `AppState` (a
real in-process `meridian-rendezvous` server, real s2s mTLS), via the exact production function
(`handle_fed_route`) Org B's real `serve_link` dispatches an inbound `FedOp::Route` frame to:
